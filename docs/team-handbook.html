Star Player of the Week

How the weekly vote works, and how your bonus turns into real money — with the actual app screens.

How the week works

Four steps, every week

STEP 1

Sign in

Open the app and sign in with your Blacfox email and password.

STEP 2

Pick a teammate

Vote for whoever went above and beyond this week. Change your mind as many times as you like before voting closes. You can't vote for yourself.

STEP 3

It stays private

Your row shows "Voted" so you know it's locked in. Nobody — not other members, not admins — can ever see who you picked.

STEP 4

The winner is revealed

An admin closes voting and reveals the result. Win on your own and your balance updates immediately. If it's a tie, more than one name shows up and each winner is paid shortly after.

Cashing out

Turning your balance into a payout

1

You ask — enter an amount up to your balance and tap Cash Out.

2

It's checked — an admin (or whoever handles finance) approves or turns it down.

3

You're paid — once approved, your balance goes down by that amount.

Questions

What if I forget to vote?

Nothing happens — there's no penalty. You can just vote next week.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen, or ask an admin for help.

Does my balance expire?

No. It stays until you cash it out.

What happens if there's a tie?

Every tied name is shown as a winner. Each of them gets paid — it just may take an admin a little longer than a solo win, which pays out instantly.
