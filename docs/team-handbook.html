Star Player of the Week

Everything you need to know, in four steps.

How it works

1

Sign in

Use your Blacfox email and password.

2

Vote for a teammate

Pick whoever went above and beyond. Change your mind anytime before voting closes. You can't pick yourself.

3

Your vote stays secret

Nobody — not other members, not admins — ever sees who you picked.

4

The winner is revealed

Win alone and you're paid instantly. If two people tie, everyone votes again between just those two — no admin has to sort it out.

Cashing out

  1. Ask — enter an amount and tap Cash Out.
  2. Wait — an admin or the finance person checks it.
  3. Get paid — your balance drops by that amount once approved.

Forgot your password?

Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen, or ask an admin.

Does my balance expire?

No — it stays until you cash it out.
