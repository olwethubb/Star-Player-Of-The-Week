The weekly cycle

Four stages, every week, in order

01

Vote

Pick one teammate who went above and beyond. Never yourself, and only once — voting again just changes your pick.

02

Stays hidden

Counts are invisible to everyone — including admins — until reveal. Nobody can see who's ahead, or who voted for whom, ever.

03

Revealed

An admin closes voting and reveals the winner. A tie means more than one name — an admin decides how it's resolved.

04

Paid

The winner's Blacfox Dollar balance goes up automatically — no admin action needed to receive it.

Who can do what

Everyone can vote. These are the extra roles layered on top.

Member

  • Cast one vote per week
  • See their own Blacfox Dollar balance
  • Request a cash-out of their balance
  • Can't see anyone's vote or the live count

Admin

  • Opens and closes voting
  • Reveals the week's result
  • Adds, renames, and removes teammates
  • Approves or rejects cash-out requests
  • Still can't see who voted for whom

Finance

  • One person at a time, assigned by an admin
  • Can approve a cash-out and lower a balance
  • Can never raise a balance
  • Doesn't get admin's other powers automatically

Owner

  • Everything an admin can do
  • Can't be demoted or removed by anyone else — including other admins
  • The one permanent safety net for the whole app

Ground rules

The four things worth knowing before you vote

One vote, any time you like

You can change your pick as many times as you want while voting is open — only your latest pick counts.

You can't vote for yourself

The app blocks it outright — your own name never appears in your own voting list.

Votes stay private forever

Not hidden until reveal — hidden permanently. Nobody, at any level, can look up who you voted for.

Ties mean more than one winner

If two or more people are tied for the top vote count, an admin decides how the bonus is handled.

Turning B$ into a payout

Cashing out a Blacfox Dollar balance

Step 1

Quick answers

Q What happens if I forget to vote?

Nothing bad — you just won't have a vote counted for that week. There's no penalty, and you can vote again next week.

Q Can I see how many people have voted so far?

Admins can see a total count while voting is open — but never who's currently ahead, and never who anyone voted for.

Q I lost my password — what do I do?

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen, or ask an admin to send you a reset link from the team panel.

Q Does my Blacfox Dollar balance expire?

No — it carries over indefinitely until you request a cash-out.
