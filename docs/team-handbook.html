Star Player of the Week

How the weekly vote works, and how your bonus turns into real money.

How the week works

Four steps, every week

STEP 1

You vote

Pick one teammate who did great work this week. You can change your mind any time before voting closes. You can't pick yourself.

STEP 2

It stays private

Nobody can see the votes while they come in — not even admins. Nobody can ever see who you voted for.

STEP 3

The winner is revealed

An admin closes the vote and shows the result. If there's a tie, more than one name will show up.

STEP 4

You get paid

If you win, your Blacfox Dollar balance goes up right away. You don't have to ask for it.

Good to know

Four quick facts

You can change your vote as many times as you want

You can never vote for yourself

Your vote is private, always — no one can look it up

A tie means more than one person wins that week

Cashing out

Turning your balance into a payout

You ask

Request a cash-out for any amount you have

→

It's checked

An admin looks at it and approves or turns it down

→

You're paid

Your balance goes down by that amount

Questions

What if I forget to vote?

Nothing happens — there's no penalty. You can just vote next week.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen, or ask an admin for help.

Does my balance expire?

No. It stays until you cash it out.
