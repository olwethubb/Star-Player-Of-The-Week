Star Player of the Week

No sign-in, no password. Everything you need to know, in five steps.

How it works

1

Pick your name

Open the app, find yourself in the dropdown, and hit Join. It's yours on this phone from then on — so make sure it's really you before you tap it.

2

Say if your stats are up or down

One tap, before you can vote. Only "Stats Up" goes on this week's poll — pick "Stats Down" and you just aren't on it yourself. You still get to vote.

3

Vote for a teammate

Pick whoever went above and beyond. Change your mind anytime before voting closes. You can't pick yourself.

4

Your vote stays secret

Who you picked isn't written down anywhere — not for teammates, not for KG, not in the database. It stays on your own phone.

5

The winner is revealed

KG closes voting and reveals the Star Player. If two people tie, everyone votes again between just those two — it sorts itself out.

Questions

Your name says "Taken" — but it's you?

That's the old phone still holding it, usually after clearing your browser or switching devices. Ask KG to free the name up, then tap it again.

New phone, or handing this one over?

Tap "Not you?" at the top. Your name goes back on the list for you to take again, and this phone forgets who it voted for. The vote itself still counts.

Can anyone see who you voted for?

No — and not because we promise not to look. It's never recorded anywhere. Not by KG, not by anyone.

No vote screen this week?

Either KG hasn't opened voting yet, or the week is paused — a holiday, say. A fresh week starts every Friday.
